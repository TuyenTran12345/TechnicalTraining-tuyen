Installation
============

* Navigate to **Apps**.
* Search with keyword *l10n_vn_viin_account_reports*.
* Press **Install**.

**Note:** To have a full feature-related observation of this module, you need to install the `Accounting app <https://viindoo.com/intro/accounting>`_.

Instructions
============

**Instruction video:** `Accounting Reports - Vietnam Accounting <https://youtu.be/get17weCRLw>`_

To view Financial reports and other accounting reports according to Circular No.200/2014/TT-BTC and Circular No.133/2016/TT-BTC, navigate to **Accounting app > Reporting**.

* `Financial report as Vietnam Accounting Standards (VAS) <https://viindoo.com/documentation/16.0/applications/finance/accounting-and-invoicing/reporting/reports/reports-according-to-vietnam-accounting-standard.html>`_:
	
  * Profit and Loss;
  * Balance Sheet;
  * Cash Flow Statement.
  * Other reports:
	
    * VAT Declaration; 
    * Account Detail Sheet; 
    * General Journal;
    * Account Cash book and Account Bank Deposit Book.
	
.. image:: 1.bao-cao-tai-chinh-theo-vas.en.jpg
   :alt: Financial report as Vietnam Accounting Standards (VAS)
   :height: 598
   :width: 1100

Otherwise, to verify the configuration of financial reports, go to **Accounting app > Configuration > Financial Reports**.

* If you know the building method related to financial reports, you can study and configure them properly.
