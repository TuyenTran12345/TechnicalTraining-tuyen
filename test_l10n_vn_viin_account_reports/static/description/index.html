Vietnam Accounting Reports (VAS)

Prebuilt Balance Sheet (B01-DN), Profit & Loss (B02-DN), Cash Flow (B03-DN), VAT declarations (01-1/GTGT, 01-2/GTGT), and required ledgers/books that comply with Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC, Circular 133/2016/TT-BTC, and Circular 119/2014/TT-BTC.

About

What this module does

Extends to_account_reports and l10n_vn_viin to provide Vietnam Accounting Standards reports required by the Ministry of Finance.

  • VAS Chart of Accounts aligned with Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC and Circular 133/2016/TT-BTC.
  • Financial statements: Balance Sheet (B01-DN), Profit & Loss (B02-DN), Cash Flow (B03-DN) for Circular 200 & 133.
  • Tax declarations: VAT listings 01-1/GTGT and 01-2/GTGT in Excel format (Circular 119/2014/TT-BTC).
  • Audit-ready ledgers and books (S38, S03a, S03b, S07, S08) with PDF/XLSX export.
