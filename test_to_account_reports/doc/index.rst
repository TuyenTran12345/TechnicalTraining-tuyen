Installation
============

#. Navigate to **Apps**.
#. Search with keyword *to_account_reports*.
#. Press **Install**.

**Note:** To have a full feature-related observation of this module, you need install `Accounting app <https://viindoo.com/intro/accounting>`_.

Instruction
===========

**Instruction video:** `Accounting Reports <https://youtu.be/mZoK3olRL00>`_

**1. View the journal-related information in the dashboard menu.**

Go to **Accounting app > Dashboard**, choose any kanban. Each kanban card is correlative with one journal in the Accounting app at this interface.

.. image:: 1.cac-thong-tin-lien-quan-den-so-nhat-ky-tren-giao-dien-kanban.en.jpg
   :alt: the journal-related information in the dashboard menu of ERP accounting software
   :height: 569
   :width: 1100

**2. View the receivable report on the customer's contact.**

Go to **Accounting app > Customers > Customers**, press **Customer Ledger**.

.. image:: 2.tong-hop-cong-no-khach-hang.en.jpg
   :alt: View the receivable report on the customer's contact
   :height: 524
   :width: 1100  

.. image:: 3.tong-hop-cong-no-khach-hang.en.jpg
   :alt: View the receivable report on the customer's contact
   :height: 562
   :width: 1100  

**3. View the accounting reports.**

Go to **Accounting app > Reports**.

* GAAP Statements:

  * Profit and Loss;
  * Balance Sheet;
  * Cash Flow Statement.

* Management reports:

  * Invoice Analysis;
  * Entries Analysis;
  * Treasury Analysis.

* Partner Reports:

  * Aged Receivable;
  * Aged Payable;
  * Partner Debt.

* Business Statements (Executive Summary).

* Audit Reports:

  * Tax Report;
  * General Ledger;
  * Trial Balance;
  * Consolidated Journals.

.. image:: 4.bao-cao-ke-toan.en.jpg
   :alt: Accounting reports
   :height: 507
   :width: 1100

* **Note:** In order to observer the financial reports according to Vietnam Accounting Standards (VAS), you need install module `l10n_vn_viin_account_reports <https://viindoo.com/apps/app/15.0/l10n_vn_viin_account_reports>`_.
