All-in-One Freight Forwarder Management

Comprehensive logistics management for freight forwarding companies, covering the complete workflow from customer inquiry, quotation, booking, shipment operations, to tracking and profitability analysis.

Key Features

End-to-End Shipment Management with HBL/MBL control

Advanced Booking & Transport Planning with schedule synchronization

Container - Package & Goods Management

Real-Time Tracking & Customer Self-Service Portal Access

Sales Integration & Revenue Optimization with pricing efficiency

Profitability Reports & Comprehensive Master Data Management

Benefits

Streamline Operations: Automate shipment workflows with unified system and smart reminders. Reduce manual data entry, minimize errors with validation rules, and standardize processes across all forwarding operations.
Enhance Customer Experience: Provide real-time tracking visibility and self-service portal for customers. Proactive status updates keep all stakeholders informed throughout the shipping journey.
Maximize Profitability: Track costs and margins per shipment to identify profitable routes and customers. Handle growing volumes without increasing operational complexity while maintaining service quality.
Ensure Compliance & Integration: Maintain proper documentation for customs, dangerous goods, and regulatory requirements. Seamlessly integrate with Sales, Accounting, and other business modules.

End-to-End Shipment Management

  • Complete shipment lifecycle management across Sea, Air, Land, and Multi-modal transport
  • Automatic numbering and HBL/MBL control
  • Flexible workflow stages
  • Real-time delivery tracking ensure on-time service

Advanced Booking & Transport Planning

  • Auto-generate bookings grouped by carrier and route from shipment packages, matching real transport legs
  • Draft multi-leg bookings aggregate ETD/ETA/Cutoff from linked routes for quick schedule checks
  • Confirmation enforces carrier/route requirements and syncs booking details back to the shipment or relevant legs

Container - Package & Goods Management

  • Three cargo input modes with container, seal, dimensions tracking
  • Complete HS Code, UN Number, DG Class management for customs compliance
  • Ultra-fast operations with cargo templates

Real-Time Tracking & Customer Portal

  • Intuitive Kanban tracking with sidebar for operations team
  • Timeline view with standardized status templates
  • Customer self-service portal for 24/7 shipment inquiry with advanced filters

Sales Integration & Revenue Optimization

  • Create logistics quotations and auto-generate shipments from sales orders
  • Full traceability linking order lines to routes
  • Supplier pricelist integration with transparent collection/disbursement marking

Reports & Comprehensive Master Data

  • Comprehensive Reporting Management with Profit Margin Analysis and Shipment Progress Tracking Across Multiple Criteria
  • Pivot/Graph reports with profitability analysis
  • Complete port, airport, route library with logistics UoM
  • Automated ETD/ETA reminders and base automation for service quality control
