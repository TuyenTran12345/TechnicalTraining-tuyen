Gia tri mang lai

Ket qua

NO 03

NO 05

Giai phap
Cong cu workflow BPMN 2.0 cho phep thiet ke truc quan, thuc thi tu dong va giam sat thoi gian thuc trong Odoo.
Van de giai quyet
Theo doi thu cong, quy tac cung nhac, thieu tam nhin quy trinh, logic phan tan va thuc thi khong nhat quan.
Thiet ke
Trinh BPMN keo tha voi tasks, gateways, events, pools, lanes, chinh sua XML va quan ly phien ban quy trinh.
Kich hoat
Khoi chay thu cong, theo su kien thay doi du lieu, hoac theo lich lap lai ngay, tuan, thang, nam.
Thuc thi
Dong co dua tren trang thai, ho tro tac vu tu dong, phan cong thu cong, cho su kien, xu ly loi va uu tien nut.
Tich hop
Gan vao moi model Odoo, chay server action, phan cong dong, su dung activity va cap nhat trang thai thoi gian thuc.
Kiem soat nang cao
Ho tro nhieu phien ban dong thoi, dinh tuyen theo domain, quy trinh con, mapping du lieu, thu lai va bo qua co phe duyet.
Giam sat
Lich su thuc thi, trinh xem trang thai, loi kem stack trace, theo doi tien do, gioi han luu giu va tu dong don dep.
Bao mat
Phan quyen theo vai tro, model va cap ban ghi, ket hop phan cong qua pools va lanes.
Ky thuat
Xay dung tren Odoo OWL voi bpmn-js, co bo kiem thu tu dong va toi uu theo mo hinh thuc thi dua tren trang thai.
