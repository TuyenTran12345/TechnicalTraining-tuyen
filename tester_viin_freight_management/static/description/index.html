Freight Charges & Local Fees
  • "Fees" menu right inside Freight to import/export surcharge definitions (CSV/Excel) without Sales/Product.
  • Bulk setup freight rates, local surcharges, collect/disburse items; apply quickly to quotations and Shipments/Bookings.
  • Control margins by syncing rate tables into cost and revenue for each lane.
Fee import/export Vendors comparison Pricelist rule
Freight Project & Operations Tasks
  • Link Shipments with Projects/Tasks; auto-link based on transport product configuration.
  • Operational task checklists per leg; reminders and notes right on the tracking interface.
  • Automatically roll timesheet costs into shipment expenses to calculate accurate profit.
Task + Stage Gantt
On-behalf Payment in Freight
  • Add "On-behalf Payments" section in the Profitability sidebar of Shipment tracking (kanban).
  • Automatically pull data from sales order lines flagged on-behalf, showing collect/disburse amounts and partners.
  • See how collect/disburse affects shipment profit without switching apps.
Freight Booking & Purchase Integration
  • Auto-link subcontractor POs (from subcontracted sale lines) to shipment routes or bookings.
  • Access related POs directly from Shipment/Booking forms to keep procurement aligned with execution.
  • End-to-end traceability: customer request → booking → vendor PO → logistics execution.
PO ↔ Booking Subcontract control
Freight & Document Integration
  • Auto-create workspaces, teams, tags, and rules in Documents to cut initial setup time.
  • Open related documents from Booking/Shipment (AWB, BL, manifest, declaration, etc.) in one click.
  • Search, permission, and version documents tied to trips to meet audits and SLAs.
